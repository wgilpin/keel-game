# Phase 07 — Station Power & Cargo Storage

**Goal:** Add Solar Panel blocks for passive power generation and Cargo Container blocks for bulk storage with terminal transfer interfaces.

## Scope

Extends the `BlockGrid`-backed station with passive solar power generation, power-scaled refining, and per-cell bulk cargo containers across `Keel.Sim` and `Keel.Game`. Adds `BlockType.SolarPanel` (hotbar slot `[4]`, cost `2` `SteelBlock`) and `BlockType.CargoContainer` (hotbar slot `[5]`, cost `2` `SteelBlock`) to `Keel.Sim.Grid`, generalizing `BlockPlacementSim` and `GrinderSim` for multi-slot block types and multi-unit `SteelBlock` costs/refunds; adds Phase 07 tuning fields (`corePowerKw`, `solarPanelPowerKw`, `refineryPowerDemandKw`, `cargoContainerPowerDemandKw`, `cargoContainerCapacity`, `cargoInteractRange`) in `config/tuning.json` and `TuningConfig`; introduces `StationPowerSim` in `Keel.Sim.Power` to compute station bus generation, active demand, and `PowerEfficiency` (`Nominal`, `Brownout`, `Unpowered`) and wires `RefinerySim.Step` to scale refining progress proportionally to `PowerEfficiency` (pausing cleanly under `RefineryStatus.NoPower` at `0 kW`); introduces `StationCargoStore` and `CargoTransferSim` in `Keel.Sim.Storage` for isolated per-cell `500`-unit `CargoContainer` inventories storing `IronOre` and `SteelBlock` reserves with non-empty deconstruction protection (`GrinderStatus.ContainerNotEmpty`); extends `WorldSaveData`, `WorldSaveSerializer`, and `WorldStateMapper` to persist `SolarPanel` and `CargoContainer` cells and per-cell `StationCargoContainers` inventories; renders distinct `2.5 m` photovoltaic `SolarPanel` and cyan-trimmed `CargoContainer` meshes/colliders in `StationGridNode`; builds `CargoTerminalModel` and the slate-cyan modal `CargoTerminalNode` UI with single-keystroke transfers (`[1]`–`[4]`, `[E]`, `[R]`, `[F]`/`[Esc]`); and surfaces live station `PWR` and `STG` readouts in the Refinery Terminal, Cargo Terminal, Suit Visor HUD, and `F3` debug telemetry overlay.

(Covered by `epic-01-solar-power-loop.md`)

(Covered by `epic-01-solar-power-loop.md`, `epic-02-cargo-container-blocks.md`, `epic-03-cargo-terminal-ui.md`)

## Not in this phase

- Dynamic player-built ship grids, ship thrusters/gyros/cockpits, or ship-mounted power/cargo (Phase 08)
- Automated conveyor item routing between `CargoContainer` blocks and `Refinery` buffers
- Complex per-wire directional power routing, batteries/accumulators, or solar raycast occlusion calculations
- New ore types (Silicon, Cobalt, Nickel) or multi-component crafting recipes (Phase 09)

## Risks

- **New-world soft-lock if station starts with zero refining power:** Requiring `SolarPanel` blocks (which cost `SteelBlock` items) before the `Refinery` can produce the player's first `SteelBlock` would deadlock a fresh world. `Core` provides `corePowerKw = 25.0 kW` auxiliary emergency power so a fresh station refines at `25%` brownout speed out of the box, motivating `SolarPanel` construction without ever soft-locking progression.
- **Item duplication or loss on multi-block deconstruction and non-empty containers:** `SolarPanel` and `CargoContainer` cost `2` `SteelBlock` items. `GrinderSim` must verify `inventory.RemainingCapacity >= 2` before allowing deconstruction and reject non-empty `CargoContainer` cells (`GrinderStatus.ContainerNotEmpty`) so neither refunded `SteelBlock`s nor stored cargo items can ever be lost.
- **Modal hotbar key collision (`1`–`4`, `E`, `R`, `F`, `Esc`):** While `CargoTerminalNode` is open, pressing `1`–`4` triggers per-item deposit/withdraw actions. `GameRoot` must consume `ShipInputReader.ConsumeSlotSelection()` inside the Cargo Terminal modal handler so transferring cargo does not simultaneously switch the player's active hotbar slot.
